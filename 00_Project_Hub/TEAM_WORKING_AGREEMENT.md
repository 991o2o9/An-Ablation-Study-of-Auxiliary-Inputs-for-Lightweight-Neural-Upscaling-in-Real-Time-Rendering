# Team Working Agreement

## Shared principles
- Each source has one primary reader; the other co-author reviews the key claims and raises questions. Rotate the primary-reader role.
- Keep source-backed statements distinct from our interpretations and hypotheses.
- Record DOI or stable URL for every source. Add page numbers to direct quotations.
- Make important scope and method decisions in `DECISION_LOG.md`, with a reason and date.
- End each work session with a written owner and next action for every open task.
- Raise disagreements early; resolve them by checking the source or running a small comparison, then record the decision.
- Use GitHub as the canonical home for manuscript, notes, and bibliography. Keep one editable LaTeX manuscript; generated PDFs are previews.
- Review each other's substantive manuscript and method changes. Agree on a section owner before editing the same LaTeX section at the same time.

- Current task list: `WEEKLY_PLAN.md`
- Expected response time for non-urgent questions: [e.g., 24 hours]
- If someone cannot meet a deadline, update the task and agree on a new date.

## Authorship and contributions
Discuss expected contributions and authorship early, revisit them as the project changes, and follow the venue's authorship policy. Do not assign authorship solely by seniority or by who writes the most words.
