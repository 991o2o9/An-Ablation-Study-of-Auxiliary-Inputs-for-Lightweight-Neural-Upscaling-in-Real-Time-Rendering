## Related Work: definition of done

- The section is written as a comparison of research themes and methods, not a sequence of isolated paper summaries.
- It covers relevant neural upscaling approaches, auxiliary or temporal inputs, and evaluation settings that inform the proposed ablation.
- Each factual claim about prior work has a verified citation; every cited key exists in `references.bib` with checked metadata.
- Important comparisons say what data, resolution, hardware, and metrics make results comparable or incomparable.
- The final paragraph explains the specific gap and why testing individual auxiliary inputs in a lightweight setting is useful, without claiming results we have not measured.
- Both authors have reviewed the text, and the PDF builds and displays the title page, Related Work, and References correctly.
